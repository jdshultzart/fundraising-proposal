04 · A Word From JD
iv
From the Artist

I started selling art in LA at 17. Forty years later, I want to give back.

For four decades, I've had the privilege of building this career in Los Angeles. My work has been collected by celebrities, corporations, music industry leaders, professional athletes, and art supporters across Southern California and beyond.

Along the way, giving back has always mattered. Through exhibitions, fundraising events, and artwork sales, my art has helped raise more than $250,000 for charitable causes and community organizations.

As I mark my 40-year anniversary, I'm expanding the gallery into something bigger: using art as a platform to raise meaningful funds and awareness for the foundations doing the work that matters. I'd love the chance to do that with you.

— JD Shultz
Founder & Artist
